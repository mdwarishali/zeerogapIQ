An enterprise insurance platform for preparing structured RFQs, coordinating insurer communication, reviewing quotation details with AI assistance and creating client-ready comparisons.

It is designed for insurance agents, insurance marketing firms, brokers, corporate insurance teams, placement teams and relationship managers.

Teams can address an RFQ to multiple insurer recipients to coordinate a submission. Incoming quotations are uploaded by your team.

The platform describes per-user official email configuration and enterprise SMTP functionality. Ask the team to confirm the setup that fits your organization.

Upload a quotation file in a supported format. AI helps identify key commercial and underwriting details for your team to review before sharing.

It can present uploaded, extracted quotation details in a standardized side-by-side view, including premiums, cover, deductibles and exclusions.

Enterprise administrators can define company details, brand colours and brochure themes for branded client-facing materials.

The product-learning assistant is designed to answer product-related questions in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

A business performance view covers RFQs, quotations, conversions, team activity, renewals and product-wise performance.
